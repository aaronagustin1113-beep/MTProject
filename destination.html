Welcome to our Beloved Municipality!

Banna, formerly known as Espiritu, is a landlocked municipality in the coastal province of Ilocos Norte. The municipality has a land area of 92.73 square kilometers or 35.80 square miles which constitutes 2.71% of Ilocos Norte's total area. Its population as determined by the 2020 Census was 19,297. This represented 3.17% of the total population of Ilocos Norte province, or 0.36% of the overall population of the Ilocos Region. Based on these figures, the population density is computed at 208 inhabitants per square kilometer or 539 inhabitants per square mile.Barangays

Paraiso

"Paraiso ti Caribquib" is a former eco-park in Barangay Caribquib, Banna, Ilocos Norte, known for its zipline and forest setting, but it has been neglected due to funding issues and typhoon damage. While efforts have been made to declare it a national tourist destination for revitalization, its current status as an active attraction is unclear, though the municipal government and local artists have recently used the site for community events like painting workshops and youth camps.

Abuos Festival

The Banna Abuos Festival in Banna, Ilocos Norte, celebrates the local tradition of harvesting red ant eggs, called abuos, as a delicacy and a symbol of unity and hard work in the community. The festival features street pageantry and dance parades that depict the diligent and collaborative nature of weaver ants and the community's perseverance, representing the spirit of timpuyog (living and growing together).

Banna Otap

True serendipity. If not for my uncle JBoy Abadilla, I wouldn’t have known Banna makes very good otap, a flaky sugared pastry. He served it at his home when we visited him today and I couldn’t wait to check out the source, right in his own backyard. A few houses away is Monette’s Bakery which is your typical corner bakery that makes tinapay. You wouldn’t normally find the otap on the shelves but in an “orocan.” Mrs. Imelda Marcos and many Bataquenos are good customers, the owner says. The Banna otap is made of crispy thin phyllo, which resembles the delicate quality of arguellanas. We all loved the lightness of this otap. Bought an “exagge” of 50 packs . When you head east, try visiting Monette’s Bakery on Zamora Street.

Banna Blend Rice Coffee

The rice coffee project in Banna, Ilocos Norte, namely in Barangay Binacag, introduced the first rice coffee to the market. The popularity of the rice coffee prompted Banna officials to approve the municipality's entrance into the DTI's "One Town, One Product" Program. Because rice coffee is made entirely of rice and has no preservatives, it is considered a healthy drink by "coffee aficionados" and has a strong market presence. The rising demand for the product necessitates future business expansion and the conquest of new and larger market segments.
